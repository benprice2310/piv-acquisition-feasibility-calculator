PIV acquisition feasibility calculator

Planning tool for two-dimensional particle image velocimetry. It turns a laser, camera, lens, seeding and flow description into the pulse separation you can actually use, and into the scales that survive correlation. Everything is computed in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Criteria

Method, thresholds and references

Symbols: s image scale in metres per pixel, M magnification, D_I interrogation window in pixels, D_w window width in metres, Δz₀ sheet thickness, U_d = U + k u′ and W_d = W + k w′ design velocities, L the structure length you want to resolve.

M = p / s
Magnification from the pixel pitch p and the image scale.
d_diff = 2.44 f# (1 + M) λ
Diffraction-limited image of a point source.
d_τ = √((M d_p)² + d_diff²)
Particle-image diameter. Below about 2 px the sub-pixel peak estimator locks to integer displacements; 2–4 px minimises the random error.
δz = 2 f# d_diff (1 + M) / M²
Depth of field. When it is thinner than the light sheet, out-of-focus particles add noise instead of signal.
ΔX = U_d Δt / s
Particle-image displacement. The quarter rule keeps it below a set fraction of the window.
N_I F_I F_O ≥ 7
Valid-detection criterion of Keane & Adrian: particle images per window, reduced by in-plane loss F_I = 1 − ΔX / D_I and out-of-plane loss F_O = 1 − W_d Δt / Δz₀. Window shifting or multi-pass correlation removes the in-plane term.
σ_U = σ_px s / Δt
Velocity resolution from the correlation uncertainty. It sets the accuracy floor on Δt and the dynamic velocity range DVR = ΔX_max / σ_px.
A/A₀ = |sinc(D_w / L)|
Amplitude of a sinusoidal structure of wavelength L surviving the window's top-hat average. Overlap adds vectors but not information.
|∂ΔX/∂x| ≤ 0.05
Displacement gradient across a window, estimated from Δu ≈ k u′ D_w / L. The classical Keane & Adrian form a = M |Δu| Δt / d_τ is reported alongside it.
St = τ_p U_d / L
Tracer Stokes number with τ_p = ρ_p d_p² / (18 μ). Settling adds a bias U_g = d_p² (ρ_p − ρ_f) g / (18 μ).
N_t = f L / U_d
PIV fields acquired while the structure convects past the plane, under Taylor's frozen-turbulence hypothesis. Two is the Nyquist limit, five a conservative planning target.

Sources: R. D. Keane and R. J. Adrian, Optimization of particle image velocimeters, Meas. Sci. Technol. 1 (1990) 1202; R. J. Adrian and J. Westerweel, Particle Image Velocimetry, Cambridge, 2011; M. Raffel et al., Particle Image Velocimetry: A Practical Guide, 3rd ed., Springer, 2018; J. Westerweel, Fundamentals of digital particle image velocimetry, Meas. Sci. Technol. 8 (1997) 1379.

  • This is an acquisition-planning tool, not a guarantee that correlation will succeed. It says nothing about calibration, illumination uniformity, reflections, timing jitter or the processing algorithm.
  • The velocity difference across a window is estimated from the design fluctuation and the structure length; substitute your own shear estimate when you have one.
  • Thresholds are conventional practice, not physical laws. Where a limit is a convention rather than a bound, the criterion says so.
